📖 HOW TO PLAY & WIN
🏁 THE OBJECTIVE & 1-LIFE PERMADEATH

Steer your high-performance racer through 99 challenging arcade stages!

  • 💀 1-Life Permadeath: No checkpoints or stage retries! One fatal crash ends your entire run and resets progress back to Level 1.
  • Stage Victory: Survive and cross the Checkered Finish Line (🏁) at the end of each track.
  • Campaign Victory: Conquer all 99 stages in a single continuous run to become the Grand Champion!
  • Track Progress: Watch the TRACK bar on the top HUD to monitor distance to the finish line.
🎮 CONTROLS & COMMANDS
WASD or ↑←↓→
Steer & Accelerate / Brake: Full 4-way dynamic vehicle movement in all stages.
SPACEBAR
Nitro Boost: Blasts forward at maximum velocity! Invulnerable to cones and oil slicks while boosting.
P or ESC
Pause / Resume: Pauses the action and brings up the race menu.
M
Toggle Audio: Mutes or unmutes synthesized engine audio and SFX.
⚡ POWER-UPS (COLLECT THESE!)
⚡ Nitro Refill: Restores 100% Nitro meter instantly.
🛡️ Energy Shield: Absorbs 1 fatal obstacle or vehicle collision.
⭐ Bonus Star: +500 score points (+25% with Gold Phantom).
⏱️ Bullet Time: Slows down hazards for 4 seconds for precision weaving.
⚠️ HAZARDS & TRAFFIC (AVOID AT ALL COSTS!)
🚧 Traffic Cones: Fatal collision unless protected by Shield or Nitro boost.
🛢️ Oil Slicks: Causes an uncontrollable 360° spinout and speed drop.
🏎️ Rival Traffic: Fast civilian and rival AI cars changing lanes!
